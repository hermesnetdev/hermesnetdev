Send & receive

Send anything, to anyone, in seconds.

Files go straight from your device to theirs, encrypted end to end. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud, and the person receiving doesn't even need the app.

Pulse Drop to a handle

Type someone's handle, or scan their Enidor QR, pick a file or a whole folder and send. They tap Accept and it arrives at full speed. Sends keep going in the background, with live progress and time left.

Scan a QR and send

No handle, no account on their side. They open enidor.com/receive and show a QR code; you scan it in Enidor, choose files and tap Send. The files open right in their browser.

Receive in any browser

Got a link or a QR from someone on Enidor? Download straight from their device in Chrome, Safari or Firefox. If the connection drops, it picks up where it left off.

Rooms with a link or code

Share files and folders as a room. Anyone with the link downloads in a browser, or joins with the code in Enidor, while your device is online. Lock it with a PIN, and take it offline whenever you like.

On your phone, too

Enidor for Android sends files or whole folders and scans QR codes with the camera. Everything you receive lands in Download/Enidor, ready to open.

Preview before you download

Play videos and music or look at photos and PDFs straight from the sender's drive, without waiting for a full download.

Security & privacy

There's no door to knock on.

The usual way to host something is to open a hole in your router and hope nobody finds it. Enidor doesn't open one at all — and nothing in the path can read what passes through.

Zero-knowledge architecture

We cannot see your files or read your messages. There is no cloud storage, no third-party database — data moves strictly from point A to point B through encrypted, direct tunnels.

Sign in with a code, not a password

Enter the one-time code we email you; there's no password or PIN to forget. Your keys stay in your device's own keychain, and a recovery code gets you back in if you lose a device.

New devices have to earn trust

A new phone or computer on your account can send files right away, but it can't manage your account until one of your devices approves it, you enter your recovery code, or 48 hours pass.

Access that ends when you say

Take a room offline or stop a send and access ends at once, even mid-download. QR sends stop sharing on their own once everything is downloaded. Remove a device and it's signed out.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

Install, sign in with your email, and send. Nothing to configure on your router, and nothing to pay for.

  1. STEP 01

    Install Enidor

    Download it for your Mac, Windows or Linux computer, or your Android phone, from the buttons above.

  2. STEP 02

    Sign in with your email

    Enter the code we email you and pick your handle, like @alex. No password, no PIN. Save the recovery code it offers.

  3. STEP 03

    Pulse Drop to a friend

    Tap +, then Pulse Drop. Type their handle or scan their QR, choose a file or folder, and send. They tap Accept.

  4. STEP 04

    Send to someone without Enidor

    Ask them to open enidor.com/receive. In Pulse Drop, choose Scan QR, scan the code on their screen, pick your files and send.

  5. STEP 05

    Create a room

    Tap +, then Create a room, and add files or folders. Copy the link or code and send it; they download in a browser, or join it in Enidor.

  6. STEP 06

    Keep track

    Transfers shows every download with progress and time left. Settings lists your devices and your recovery code.
