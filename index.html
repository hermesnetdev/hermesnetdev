Features

Everything on your other computer, one click away.

Pair your devices once. After that there's nothing to upload, sync or copy.

Mesh Vault

Click any paired device that's online to mount its vault and browse its drive. No room codes, no manual transfers.

Virtual volumes

Downloads, Documents, Desktop and Pictures are mapped from the start. Add an external drive, a repo or any folder with one click.

QR code pairing

Show a QR code on one device and scan it from the other with the built-in scanner, using a webcam or an image file.

Multi-tab workspace

Browse several devices and guest rooms side by side. Directory caching makes switching between tabs instant.

Guest rooms

Share up to five folders at once with anyone, using 6-digit room codes. Revoke access with one click or by closing the app.

Stream before you download

Watch videos, play audio and view images straight from the host over the tunnel, full screen included.

True peer-to-peer speed

Files move directly between devices, so your network is the only limit. No cloud storage quotas or upload queues.

Automatic updates

New versions download in the background and install themselves. You only ever download Enidor once.

Security & privacy

Nobody in the middle can read your files. That includes us.

Enidor was built zero-trust from the start. Your files stay on your drives and only ever travel between devices you chose.

  • Paired devices only

    Opening a vault requires an Ed25519 cryptographic handshake. Only devices you've explicitly paired can see or request files.

  • A relay that can't see

    When strict NAT forces traffic through our relay, it acts as a dumb pipe. Transfers are encrypted in transit, so the relay can't read them.

  • Mutual revocation

    Lost a device? Unpair it and the tunnel closes immediately. Trust is deleted on both ends and access is gone for good.

  • No cloud storage

    Files are never uploaded to a middleman database. They move from point A to point B and nowhere else.

  • Strictly scoped access

    Enidor only reads the folders you mount or share. The rest of your disk stays locked down.

Get started

Up and running in four steps.

  1. 01

    Download

    Get the build for your computer from the releases below. Install it on every device you want in your mesh.

  2. 02

    Pair your devices

    On the first device, click the phone icon in the sidebar to show a QR code. On the second, click + and scan it. Name the device and you're paired.

  3. 03

    Add folders to your vault

    Under Mounted Vault Folders, click + to add any folder, drive or project. Paired devices can open it right away.

  4. 04

    Share with a guest

    Click + Share New Folder and send the 6-digit code. Your friend enters it in Remote Explorer. Access ends when you click Stop or quit.
