Why Enidor

You've been paying rent to store your own files.

The Sovereign Ecosystem

One handle. Every device.

Bind your hardware to a Global Handle like @alex. Once each device is authenticated with your authenticator app and Ecosystem PIN, they form an invisible, high-speed matrix — every drive one click away from every other.

  • Universal access

    Click any device that's online to mount its vault instantly. No uploads, no sync folder, no waiting.

  • Unified presence

    A file on your desktop at home opens on your laptop across the world — same mesh, zero configuration.

  • No public IP required

    Nothing is exposed to the internet. Your devices dial out to each other instead of waiting to be found.

Direct Peer Matrix

Send a file without giving it away.

Search a colleague's Global Handle, forge a permanent encrypted tunnel, and talk. When you attach a file, nothing is uploaded — Enidor opens a Micro-Room: a private tunnel to that exact file, still sitting on your drive.

  • Granular time-to-live

    Grant access for 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days — or never expire. You choose at the moment you send.

  • Instant revocation

    Click Revoke and the tunnel is severed and the file locked, even mid-download on their end.

  • Messages nobody else holds

    Chat runs on the same point-to-point infrastructure as your files. There's no inbox on a server to subpoena, breach or mine.

Sharing

Two ways to let someone in. Both temporary.

Not everyone belongs in your permanent address book. Enidor gives you throughput without a relationship, and rooms that evaporate the moment you close them.

Pulse Drop

Beam massive files straight to someone you've never linked with. They get a prompt, they accept, and the transfer runs at full network speed — ephemeral, direct, nothing left behind.

Guest Rooms

Host up to five local folders at once behind ephemeral 6-digit codes. Hand the code to a group, they dock into exactly those folders, and access evaporates when you close the room.

Security & privacy

There's no door to knock on.

The usual way to host something is to open a hole in your router and hope nobody finds it. Enidor doesn't open one at all — and nothing in the path can read what passes through.

Zero-knowledge architecture

We cannot see your files or read your messages. There is no cloud storage, no third-party database — data moves strictly from point A to point B through encrypted, direct tunnels.

MFA and a cryptographic PIN

Your identity is held by an authenticator app (TOTP) plus a local Ecosystem PIN. Without the PIN, the vault on that machine stays cryptographically scrambled.

Emergency kill switch

Under threat? One click in the Security Center drops every inbound and outbound tunnel across all zones, severing the machine from the outside world.

Revocation that's already done

Unlink a device or revoke a Micro-Room and the tunnel closes immediately — active downloads fail, trust is deleted on both ends, access is gone for good.

Get started

Six steps to your own mesh.

Install, claim your handle, and add devices. Nothing to configure on your router, and nothing to pay for.

  1. STEP 01

    The genesis node

    Launch Enidor on your main computer, claim your Global Handle, scan the QR code with your authenticator app, save your recovery codes, and set the Ecosystem PIN that decrypts your local vault.

  2. STEP 02

    Expand the mesh

    Install Enidor on a second machine, sign in with your handle, confirm with your authenticator and PIN. Open My Ecosystem and both devices are there, ready to browse.

  3. STEP 03

    Forge peer links

    In My Peers, search a colleague's handle and hit Forge Link. Once they accept, a permanent encrypted chat tunnel exists between you.

  4. STEP 04

    Attach, with an expiry

    Open a chat, click the paperclip, and pick how long they get access. Change your mind at any point and hit Revoke Access on the message.

  5. STEP 05

    Pulse Drop a stranger

    Look up a handle you don't want to keep, choose Pulse Drop instead of a link, and send. They accept the prompt and the file moves at full speed.

  6. STEP 06

    Stay in control

    The shield icon opens Security & Settings: active sessions, themes, the kill switch, and Logout & Unlink to erase the vault keys from that hardware.
